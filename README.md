# GSPro Control Box 365

A mobile and tablet remote control interface for the GSPro Golf Simulator. Control your game, map your bag, and manage your OBS stream cameras directly from your hitting mat.

## Quick Start

1. **Download:** Go to the [Releases](https://github.com/Mickey202505/gspro-control-box/releases) section and download `GSProControlBox_Setup_v1.05.exe`.
2. **Install:** Run the setup on your GSPro PC.
3. **Run:** Launch the GSPro Control Box from your Desktop.
4. **Connect:** 
   - Ensure your tablet/phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.
   - Scan the QR code displayed in the server window with your mobile device.
   - The control interface will open in your mobile browser.

## Key Features
- **Full Gameplay Control:** Mulligans, Aiming, Camera views, and more.
- **Bag Mapping:** Save your clubs and distances for a synced experience.
- **OBS Integration:** Switch camera scenes for your overhead or side-view cameras.
- **Practice Mode:** Interactive touch-pad to move aim points on the mini-map.
